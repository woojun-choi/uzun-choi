Three black-and-white film photographs shot while traveling Jeju.

Sanjeong (山頂) — A chance encounter with Sanbangsan, its massive rock face seeming to surge forward. Shot in black and white to reveal the cliff's texture and sheer weight through contrast rather than color. The title draws on a Jeju legend that Sanbangsan is the summit of Hallasan, cut off and cast away.

Sosaekchaebon — A scene stumbled upon at a café called Sosaekchaebon during a family trip: hand-dyed handkerchiefs fluttering in the spring wind beyond the window, framed exactly as seen from inside.

Rapeseed, Overflowing — An endless rapeseed field rendered in black and white, so viewers would imagine its yellow for themselves. The hope was that spring's fullness would come through more vividly once the color was stripped away.
