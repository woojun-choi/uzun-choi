A survival typing game. You're given a pair of Korean initial consonants (ㄱㄴ, ㅇㅂ …) and keep typing real words that match before the 60-second timer runs out. A correct word adds 3 seconds, a word that isn't in the dictionary costs one, and the clock keeps speeding up. When it's over, the game shows the words you missed — the "oh, that one too!" moment that makes you play again — and tapping any found or missed word brings up its dictionary definition.

The second title in Tiny Game Heaven. Dictionary data from the National Institute of Korean Language is processed at build time and shipped as static files, and the game handles problems unique to Korean typing, like input arriving mid-composition in the Hangul IME. The same code also ships as an iOS and iPad app, released on the App Store in November 2026 as "ㄱㄴ - 초성 게임".
