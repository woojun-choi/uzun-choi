A grid of keycaps fills the screen, each showing one Hangul syllable. Drag a box over keys that spell a real dictionary word (사 + 과 → 사과, "apple") and they pop out and disappear. Clear as many keys as you can before time runs out — or take on the Daily Puzzle, the same board for everyone each day.

The third title in Tiny Game Heaven. The name comes from 천·지·인 (·, ㅡ, ㅣ), the heaven-earth-human principle behind Hangul vowels, and from the classic Cheonjiin phone keypad; the look reimagines a Hangul keyboard in 1-bit pixel graphics. From the same code as the web version, I built iOS and iPad apps and released them on the App Store in November 2026 as "천지인 - 한글 단어 퍼즐." I also designed the app-only parts, such as storage that keeps your records safe on the device and haptic feedback.
