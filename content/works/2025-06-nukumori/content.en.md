Nukumori (ぬくもり) is a site-specific work that began with the discovery of repair traces on a net at a futsal court.

The hand-tied knots on the torn net felt like more than just a simple fix — they were acts of care and maintenance that sustained the space. Respecting those gestures, the repairs were continued with white rope, aiming to express a story where time, touch, and place are intimately connected. Handled the repair work, photography, and documentation independently.
