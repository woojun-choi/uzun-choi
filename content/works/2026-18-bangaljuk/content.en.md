A one-gesture precision game: drag a single straight line to cut an object exactly in half. The object splits along your line, and the game shows the ratio of the two pieces (say, 48.3 : 51.7) along with an S–C grade. Five rounds make a game, and the result is ready to share.

The first title in Tiny Game Heaven, a collection of small web games built around Hangul and Korean humor. No sign-up and no tutorial — you start playing from the first screen, and everything runs in the browser with no server. Planning, graphics, Canvas rendering and the cut-judging logic were all done by me.
